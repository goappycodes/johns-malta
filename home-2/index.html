          Real people in Ħamrun answer the phone, day and night.

          What happens if my flight is delayed?

          We track every flight number you give us. Your pickup time moves with the actual landing time and the first 60 minutes after landing are free, so there is nothing to call or change.

          Where will my driver meet me?

          In the arrivals hall at Malta International Airport, holding a board with your name. You will receive the driver's name, phone number and car plate by email before pickup.

          Can I cancel or change my booking?

          Yes. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before pickup, and changes are free up to 48 hours before. Use Manage booking with your booking code and email.

          Do you provide child seats?

          Yes. Add child and booster seats while booking and they will be fitted before pickup, for €5 each.

          How do I pay, and do I get a VAT invoice?

          Pay online by Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay or Google Pay. All prices are in euro and include VAT. A receipt is emailed instantly, and you can add company details for a VAT invoice.

          How does a wedding car deposit work?

          Check your date, then pay a €150 deposit online to hold the car. The balance is due 14 days before the wedding. We call you to plan timings and routes well before the day.
