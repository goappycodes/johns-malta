Where will I meet my airport driver?+

Your chauffeur meets you in the arrivals area. Your confirmation includes meeting instructions and the team’s contact details. Add your flight number when booking so your arrival details stay with your journey.

Can I book a return transfer?+

Yes. Choose “Return trip” in the airport transfer form and add your return date and time. Both journeys appear in your booking summary before checkout.

Which vehicle should I choose?+

Compare passenger and luggage capacity before booking. For child seats, extra luggage, or accessibility requirements, add your needs in the journey details or contact our team to confirm a suitable vehicle.

How do payments and confirmations work?+

The proposed booking experience supports card, Apple Pay and Google Pay through Trust Payments, subject to merchant setup and device availability. You see your EUR total and VAT breakdown before payment, with confirmation and receipt by email. This design preview does not process payments.

Can I change or cancel my booking?+

Use “My booking” with your booking reference and email to request help. Cancellation and amendment terms are shown before you confirm, and may differ for transfers, tours and wedding cars. Final policies will be confirmed with John’s.
