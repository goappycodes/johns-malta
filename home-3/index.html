            Questions? Ask Ħamrun.

            What happens if my flight is delayed?

            We track your flight. Pickup moves with the real landing time and the first 60 minutes of waiting are free.

            Where will my driver meet me?

            In the arrivals hall at Malta International Airport, holding a board with your name. You get the driver's name, phone and plate before pickup.

            Can I cancel or change my booking?

            Yes. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before pickup, and changes are free up to 48 hours before, in Manage booking.

            Do you have child seats?

            Yes. Add child or booster seats while booking and they are fitted before pickup, for €5 each.

            How do I pay, and do I get a VAT receipt?

            Pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Prices are in euro with VAT included, and your receipt is emailed straight away.
